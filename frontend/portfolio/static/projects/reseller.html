04 Mobile app · Commerce

Buy. Sell. Manage.
All in one place.

A reseller operating system, built under the working name Vendora: inventory, profit, invoices and payments for people who sell sneakers, electronics and clothing — run entirely from a phone.

  • Platform iOS & Android
  • Built with React Native · Expo 56
  • Backend FastAPI · PostgreSQL
  • Status Pre-release

Real screenshots from the application. Catalogue and figures shown are test data.

The problem

Most resellers run on a spreadsheet and a good memory.

Stock lives in one place, sales in another, and profit is worked out at the end of the month — if at all. Every platform has its own export, and none of them agree. The work happens standing up, at a market or a counter, on a phone.

The approach

One item record, everything hangs off it.

An item carries its photos, condition, buy price, asking price and platform. From that single record the app derives profit and loss per item and across the portfolio, builds invoices, and reconciles against a connected point of sale — so the numbers are a consequence of the inventory rather than a second thing to maintain.

  • Inventory

    Photos, barcode scanning, condition, buy and sell price, platform.

  • Profit engine

    Real-time P&L per item and across the portfolio.

  • Invoices

    Build a PDF invoice from inventory and send it to the buyer.

  • Payments

    Stripe-powered checkout and subscription billing.

  • Lightspeed sync

    Pull sales and stock, publish catalogue changes, disconnect safely.

  • Market pricing

    Compare UPC retail offers against the seller's own sales history.

Stack

Verified from the project's own README and source tree.

Where it stands

Pre-release, in readiness review. The core flows — inventory, quick sale, invoicing and sync — are built and exercised, with continuous integration running over the backend and the mobile app. The public name is not final, which is why it appears on the homepage as “Reseller App”.
