About

I build the thing
that fixes it.

I’m Lex Santiago. I build software for small businesses — point-of-sale, mobile apps, websites, AI systems — then put it live and keep it running. Usually all four, because a business with a problem rarely needs just one of them.

  • Based in New Jersey
  • Studied at NJIT
  • Currently Head of Technology, IBDS
  • Also IT technician

What I actually do

Not one product. Whatever the problem needs.

The AI Receptionist is the piece people know, and it is a good piece — but it is one product, not the job. The job is closer to this: a bakery counts stock by hand and prints labels all day, so I write a point-of-sale in .NET that fits how they already work. A reseller runs a business out of a spreadsheet, so I build a phone app with real profit tracking. A company needs its sites, hosting and internal systems to hold together, so I run that.

Most of it starts the same way: something is slow, manual, or falling through the cracks. The interesting part is working out what actually fixes it — which is sometimes software, and sometimes just a network cable in the right port.

How I work

  • Say no when it fits

    If software is not the answer, or I am not the right person, I would rather tell you than take the work.

  • Ship early, not perfect

    You see it running while it is still cheap to change. A demo at the end helps nobody.

  • Own it after launch

    Deployed, backed up, monitored. Things break; the difference is whether someone is watching.

  • Keep it yours

    Your data, your accounts, no lock-in. If you want to take it elsewhere, you can.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Anything I build for you runs on accounts you own, with the smallest set of permissions that does the job. Where a product touches a third-party API, it follows that platform’s compliance rules rather than working around them. This site is self-hosted and stores only what you deliberately send it — read the privacy policy for the detail.
